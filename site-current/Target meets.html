Target meets

Birmingham Masters Swimming Club have a mixture of competitive and non-competitive members. Whilst competition is not a requirement for members, swim meets are a great opportunity to reach personal goals and make all that ploughing up and down worthwhile, as well as getting to know your fellow team mates.

As a club we ‘target’ the 'A' races listed below and also have a list of ‘other’ or 'B' meets which are very popular to our members also.

All meets and entry requirements are advertised on the Swim England website, and can be found here.
