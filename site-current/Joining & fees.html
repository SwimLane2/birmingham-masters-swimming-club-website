Thinking about joining us?

Looking for a club to join? Come and and try a session and see if we're right for you.

Just send us an email and we will let you know the best/quietest session that week and we can also make sure you have access to the pool.

As with all clubs, you will need to fill out an insurance form which covers you for the duration of the swim, and these will be available on the poolside before jumping in.

Under our insurance, you can do a maximum of 3 trial sessions before you need to join the club. These are pay-as-you-go at £6 per session, payable into the club bank account by electronic transfer before the swim. Details available - use the 'contact us' button below.

We typically swim between 2000 and 3000 meters in an hour depending upon your fitness, level and ability. Please see below for some sample sessions.

Fees

An annual subscription will be charged to all members in 2025, as Birmingham Masters is a non-profit making club, run by its members, with fees covering pool and coaching costs. The annual membership fee is usually collected once a year in January. Please note that a £10 admin fee will also be charged for late payments of membership fees in relation to those who are members at 1 January 2025.

In addition, for insurance purposes, members are also required to hold Swim England membership. This should also be paid through the club together with the annual membership fee, unless you pay this through a different club.

New members can pay solely on a per session basis for the maximum of 3 sessions before they would be required to officially join the club to continue swimming.

Training ettiquette

It may have been a long time since you last swam with a club, or maybe you have never swum with a club at all - what is the polite and friendly way to swim?

These are some guidelines that help us all train together:

Before you get in the water
  • Help us get the lane ropes in place at the start of, and put them away again at the end of, the session - everyone lends a hand!
  • Mark your attendance on the register sheet, by either 'ticking' next to or writing your name at the bottom of the sheet - available on poolside ahead of the swim.
  • Check which way your lane is swimming (clockwise or anti-clockwise) and follow suit, stopping painful collisions of arms in adjoining lanes
  • Even if you're an older version of Leon Marchand, please don't dive into the pool at the shallow end - it is very dangerous.
Now let's get started
  • Start at the back of the lane, unless you know you're quicker than your fellow swimmers.
  • If you are being held up ask to go ahead, one person at a time, until you are not holding anyone up or catching the swimmer in front.
  • Please be respectful - the aim is not to try to catch the person in front of you, but to push yourself throughout the entire set.
  • A light tap on the toes will let you know if the person behind you has caught you up, if this happens pause at the end of the length to allow the swimmer behind to go in front.
  • A firm favourite - always leave a five second gap between you and the swimmer in front when you set off.
  • In the unfortunate event of a clash with another swimmer in the water (maybe a clash of knuckles or an accidental kick) keep swimming, but apologise at the end of the length and check they are OK - no-one means to do it on purpose!
  • If you need to get out before the end of the session, please indicate to the coach as they will be concerned about your welfare.

Just following these simple guidelines will help us all to enjoy our training sessions so much more.

Thank you!
