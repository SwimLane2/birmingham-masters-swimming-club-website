Pool locations

King Edward's School Swimming Pool ('KES') Edgbaston Park Road, Edgbaston, Birmingham, England, B15 2UA

DIRECTIONS: Drive in through the entrance on the Bristol Road, follow the road round to the parade ground carpark. The boys' pool is facing the rugby pitches.

University of Birmingham Swimming Pool ('Uni') Sport & Fitness, University of Birmingham, Edgbaston, Birmingham, B15 2TT

DIRECTIONS: Once you have joined Birmingham Masters you will be issued with a card and locker wristband at the university reception, allowing you to park in the sports centre carpark and giving you access to the pool.

Sandwell Aquatics Centre ('Sandwell') Londonderry Lane, Smethwick, B67 7EW

DIRECTIONS: Plenty of parking onsite. Turn right through the main entrance to the reception desk. No cards needed, walk down the stairs to the changing rooms. Lockers are £1 refundable.
